# How to Use the Resilient Wealth Group CRM

**A guide for the team. Wealth, Conducted with Purpose.**

This is where you work your assigned leads. Your job is simple to say and hard to do well: take each lead from the first phone call to a kept appointment, log what happens along the way, and hand it off when there is an opportunity. The names in here are real people who raised their hand at our seminars. Treat them with care, and never discuss specifics outside the firm.

> **Before you start:** the CRM is at **https://crm.yourresilientwealth.com/**. Carlos will set up your login. Use a desktop or tablet for the best experience (it works on a phone but is not built for it).

---

## 1. Getting in

**First time (request access):**
1. Go to **https://crm.yourresilientwealth.com/**.
2. Click the **Request access** tab.
3. Enter your full name, email, and create a password (at least 6 characters).
4. Click **Request access**. You will see: *"Request sent. You'll be able to sign in once the owner approves your account."*
5. Carlos approves you. Then you can sign in.

**Every time after that:** use the **Sign in** tab with your email and password.

Forgot your password? Ask Carlos to send a reset link from the Team page.

---

## 2. Your four screens

| Screen | What it is | Use it to |
|---|---|---|
| **My Board** | Your leads as cards in columns by stage | See your whole pipeline and drag leads forward |
| **My Leads** | Your leads as a sortable table | Sort, filter, and find any lead fast |
| **Today's Queue** | Your daily to-do list | Know exactly who to work today |
| **My Stats** | Your weekly numbers | See your dials, reaches, appointments, and reach rate |

The stages, left to right: **New → Attempting → Reached → Appointment Set → Appointment Kept.**

---

## 3. Your daily routine

Start in **Today's Queue** every day and work it top to bottom:
1. **Upcoming appointments** first. These are booked. Protect them.
2. **Callbacks to follow up.** People who asked you to call back.
3. **Fresh leads to work (best first).** New leads, highest quality at the top.

Work the best leads first. A **GOLD** or **HIGH** lead deserves your time before a **LOW** one.

---

## 4. Working a lead, step by step

1. **Open the lead.** Click the card (My Board) or the name (My Leads). The detail panel opens on the right.
2. **Call them.** Tap the phone number to dial.
3. **Log the activity.** In the **Log activity** section:
   - Pick the type: **Call**, **Text**, **Email**, **Voicemail**, or **Other**.
   - Set the **Disposition** (the outcome): *Left Voicemail, No Answer, Bad Number, Not Interested, Call Back, Reached (pitched), Appointment Set, Unable to Reach.*
   - Check **"Counts as a reach"** if you actually spoke with them.
   - Add a quick note.
   - Click **＋ Log it**. Your attempts count updates automatically.
4. **Book the appointment.** When they agree to meet, click **Set Appointment**, pick the date and time, and click **Confirm appointment**. The lead moves to **Appointment Set**.
5. **After the meeting,** open the lead and click **Mark Appointment Kept**.
6. **Close it out.** Choose **Opportunity Opened** (there is business to pursue, it hands off to the next step and your job here is done) or **No Opportunity** (nothing to pursue).

That full path, every time: **Call → Log it → Reached → Set Appointment → Appointment Kept → Opportunity Opened.**

---

## 5. Reading your leads

- **Tier badge:** GOLD, HIGH, MEDIUM, LOW. This is the lead's quality score. Work the top tiers first.
- **Why this score:** a short explanation (age, years of service, plan type, salary) so you know what you are working with before you dial.
- **Returning attendee** banner: this person has been to a seminar before. Check the history and previous notes.

---

## 6. Finding things fast

- **Search box** (top): type a name, employer, or phone number.
- **Column filters** (the small arrow on a table header): sort and filter your list. Use **Clear all** to reset.

---

## 7. The rules (please follow these)

**Do:**
- **Log every attempt.** Even a voicemail. Your stats, and the team scorecard, are built from what you log. If it is not logged, it did not happen.
- **Keep stages current.** Drag leads forward as they progress.
- **Book appointments in the CRM,** not just in your head or your phone.

**Do not:**
- You cannot see or take another agent's leads. You only work what is assigned to you.
- You cannot upload lists, delete leads, or reassign them. That is the owner's job.
- If a lead looks wrong, is a duplicate, or has a bad number you cannot fix, tell Carlos.

---

## 8. Your job vs. the owner's job

| You (agent) | Carlos (owner) |
|---|---|
| Work your assigned leads | Uploads and assigns leads |
| Log calls, texts, emails | Approves new agents |
| Set and keep appointments | Sees all leads and team reports |
