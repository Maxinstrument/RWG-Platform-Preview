Two shapes, and the difference is whether the controls are a form or a sentence. A form may wrap onto a second row; a sentence may not, because a wrapped sentence stops reading as one.

Notes

Every place in the CRM where you write something about a person, a family or a case is this one control — the opportunity’s Details, a household’s Notes, a call log, the Home composer. Build it with RWG.ui.noteEditor({id, value, placeholder}) and read it back with noteRead(id) (HTML) or noteText(id) (the words alone). Never hand-roll a <textarea> for a note.
